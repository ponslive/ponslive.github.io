How I build

  1. Scale first

    Architect for 10x before you need it. A system that crumbles under growth isn’t a product — it’s a prototype with a deadline.

  2. Data as a product

    Every system should produce insight, not just output. Telemetry, logs and metrics are first-class deliverables.

  3. Ship, then refine

    Velocity and quality are not opposites. Real users surface real problems; no internal review matches production.
